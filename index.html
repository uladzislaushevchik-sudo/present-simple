утверждения · отрицания · вопросы · to be

Present Simple — когда?

Привычки и регулярные действия
I go to work every day. · She drinks coffee in the morning.
Факты и общее
Water boils at 100°C. · He lives in Minsk.

Формы (обычные глаголы)

+
I / you / we / they work
he / she / it works
−
I / you / we / they don’t work
he / she / it doesn’t work
?
Do I / you / we / they work?
Does he / she / it work?
-s / -es / -ies
work → works · go → goes
watch → watches · study → studies

to be (am / is / are)

+
I am · he/she/it is
you/we/they are
−
I’m not · isn’t · aren’t
?
Am I…? · Is he…? · Are you…?
Без do/does
не: *Does he is* · *Do you are*
